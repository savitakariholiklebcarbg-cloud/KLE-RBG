About the College

Quality education for the rapidly evolving IT industry

KLE's Malagouda Patil BCA is a distinguished institution under the umbrella of the Karnataka Lingayat Education Society (KLE Society), renowned for its commitment to quality education in Computer Applications. Located on Diggewadi Road, near the BEO Office in Raibag Rural, Karnataka, the college offers a Bachelor of Computer Applications programme that prepares students for careers in the fast-moving world of technology.

Established in 2023 with a vision to expand the horizons of teaching and learning, the college nurtures both the interpersonal and intrapersonal disposition of every student — backed by a dedicated faculty and infrastructure built for hands-on, practical learning: lab sessions, projects and internships that turn theory into industry-relevant experience.

Rooted in the KLE Society's century-long legacy of education across Karnataka
